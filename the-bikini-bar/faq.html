Questions, answered

Everything you'd ask the bartender before ordering.

How long does a made-to-order suit take?

Every suit is cut and sewn after you order it. Production takes 7–10 business days, and shipping adds 2–5 more depending on where you are. We'll email you tracking the moment it leaves the studio.

How do I find my size?

Tops and bottoms are sized independently, so measure both. Grab a soft tape measure, check your bust and hip measurements against the chart in the builder, and when you're between sizes we recommend sizing up for bottoms and following the chart for tops. Still unsure? Message us — fit advice is our favorite part of the job.

Can I order a top and bottom in different sizes?

Yes — that's the whole point of the bar. Every top and bottom is its own item with its own size, so a small top with large bottoms (or vice versa) is completely normal here.

What's your return policy?

Because every suit is made just for you, we can't restock returns. If your suit arrives with a defect or we got your order wrong, we'll remake it free of charge. If the fit isn't right, contact us within 14 days and we'll work out an exchange on the piece that doesn't fit.

Where do you ship?

Currently the US and Canada, with free standard shipping on orders over $75. International shipping is on the roadmap — join the mailing list and we'll let you know when your country comes online.

How should I care for my suit?

Rinse in cold fresh water after every wear, hand wash with mild soap, and lay flat to dry in the shade. Skip the washing machine, the dryer, and the hot tub jets — your suit will thank you with years of service.

Do you offer gift cards?

We do — from $25 to $200, delivered by email with a note from you. It's the easiest way to give someone a custom suit without guessing their size.

Can my combination end up on the menu?

Absolutely. Tag us in a photo of your mix on Instagram or TikTok. Each season we add community favorites to the house specials list — named by the person who invented them.
