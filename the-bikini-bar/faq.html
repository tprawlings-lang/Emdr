Help

Questions, answered.

Everything you'd ask before you build.

How long does my build take?

Every bikini is cut and sewn after you order it. Production takes 7–10 business days, and shipping adds 2–5 more depending on where you are. You'll get tracking the moment it leaves the studio.

How do I find my fit?

Tops and bottoms are sized independently, so measure both. Check your bust and hip measurements against the size guide in the builder. Between sizes? Size up for bottoms and follow the chart for tops. Still unsure — message us. Fit advice is the best part of this job.

Can my top and bottom be different sizes?

Yes — that's the whole point of the bar. Every top and bottom is its own piece with its own size. A small top with large bottoms is completely normal here.

How do charms work?

Charms clip onto your ties and hardware, so you can add, remove, and swap them anytime. Pick them in Step 4 of the builder, or add more later from the Charm Bar. They're $6 each.

What's your return policy?

Because every build is made just for you, we can't restock returns. If your suit arrives with a defect or we got your build wrong, we'll remake it free. If the fit isn't right, contact us within 14 days and we'll work out an exchange on the piece that doesn't fit.

Where do you ship?

The US and Canada, with free standard shipping over $75. International is on the roadmap — join the email list and you'll be the first to know.

How do I care for my suit?

Rinse in cold fresh water after every wear, hand wash with mild soap, and lay flat to dry in the shade. Unclip your charms before washing. Skip the washing machine, the dryer, and the hot tub jets.

Do you do bachelorette parties and events?

We do — it's our favorite thing. We bring the full bar to your party or resort: tops, bottoms, ties, and the charm station. Bring The Bikini Bar to your party.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes — $25 to $200, delivered by email with a note from you. The easy gift when you know her taste but not her size.
