Behind the bar

Why we decided swimwear should work like a good cocktail menu.

It started with a mismatched suit

The Bikini Bar began the way most good ideas do: with a problem. The top that fit never came with bottoms that did, the color you loved never came in your cut, and "the perfect suit" always seemed to belong to someone with different measurements.

So we tore up the rulebook and rebuilt swimwear like a bar menu. You pick the cut, you pick the color, you pick the size — for the top and the bottom independently. We cut and sew every order individually, which means no warehouse of suits hoping to fit "average," and no waste from over-production.

Every body is a different recipe. We just keep the bar stocked.

What we believe

Made to order, always

Nothing is sewn until you order it. That keeps quality high, waste low, and means your size is never "sold out."

Mix-and-match by default

Tops and bottoms are sold separately in independent sizes, because bodies don't come in matched sets.

You're the mixologist

We'll suggest house specials, but the best combination is the one you invent. The menu is just the starting point.
