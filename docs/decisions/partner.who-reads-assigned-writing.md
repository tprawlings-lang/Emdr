## The question

For the partner's clinical lead: what a person writes in a clinician-assigned practice is readable by them and by the clinician who assigned it. Should anyone else on the care team be able to read it?

## What happens while this is unanswered

Only the person and the assigning clinician. A colleague who opens it is told whose it is and shown nothing; the distress flag still reaches the care team's queue, without the words.

## The choices

### A. Assigning clinician only  — *suggested*

As the handoff says: the person and the one clinician who gave them the practice.

*What would change:* Nothing changes.

### B. Their whole care team

Anyone caring for the person can read it, for continuity when the assigning clinician is away.

*What would change:* Every clinician on the person's care team can open the writing; each read is still recorded.
